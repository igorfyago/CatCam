How CatCam compares

DroidCam, Iriun and Camo are good, mature products; people use them happily. The difference is the model. CatCam has no paid tier, so there is nothing to unlock: no watermark to remove, no resolution to buy back, no subscription. The code is public.

PriceWatermarkWhat free gets youAccount or cloudSource
CatCam Free, entirely None Everything: Wi-Fi + USB, mic, zoom, night mode None, video stays on your network Open, GPL-2.0
DroidCam Free at SD, Pro in-app purchase On HD until Pro Standard-definition video, core features No account needed Closed
Iriun Free, one-time Pro unlock Small logo until Pro All connection modes, watermarked video No account needed Closed
Camo Free tier, Pro subscription or lifetime Overlay controls sit in Pro Up to 720p Optional Closed

Checked 2026-08 against each vendor's own site; details move, their pages are authoritative. Fairness note: all three support iPhone and macOS, which CatCam does not. CatCam is Windows 11 + Android, and younger.
