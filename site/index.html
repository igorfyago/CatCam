CatCam

Turn a spare tablet into your PC's webcam.

Download for Windows Get the tablet app

Windows 11 (22H2 or newer) · Android 8+ · both on the same Wi-Fi, or a USB cable

1 Install the PC app Unzip and run the installer on your Windows PC. A cat appears in your system tray.
2 Install the tablet app Put CatCam on the spare tablet and press the big button.
3 They find each other Same network is enough. "CatCam" shows up as a camera in Teams, Zoom and everything else.

No cloud. No accounts.

Your video goes from the tablet straight to your own PC over your own network, and nowhere else. There are no servers, no sign-ups, no analytics, and the whole thing is open source, so you can check. Privacy policy · Source on GitHub

CatCam is free and open source (GPL-2.0) · github.com/igorfyago/CatCam
